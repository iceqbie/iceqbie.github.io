Profile

I'm a graduate student working on astrophysical plasma physics and numerical simulations.

My research looks at plasma processes in astrophysical environments through theoretical analysis and computational modeling. Lately I'm testing whether machine-learning closure models can stand in for expensive kinetic simulations.

Interests

01

How Turbulence Divides Its Energy

In a collisionless plasma nothing makes ions and electrons share energy. What sets the ion-to-electron heating ratio, as a function of plasma β and Tᵢ/Tₑ? It decides what an accretion flow around a black hole looks like.

  • Turbulence
  • Ion/Electron Heating
  • β
02

Heat or a Nonthermal Tail

The same energy can thermalise or end up in a power-law tail of fast particles. How much goes where, and what sets the slope? The answer is a fitted distribution function with an honest error bar.

  • Particle Acceleration
  • Distribution Functions
03

Which Mechanism Dissipates

Landau damping, stochastic heating, reconnection in the thin sheets turbulence makes, or the firehose and mirror instabilities. Each predicts a different split, and which one wins depends on β and scale.

  • Landau Damping
  • Reconnection
  • Instabilities
04

Can the Simulation Be Trusted?

Standard explicit PIC heats the plasma numerically. How much of a published heating fraction survives a change of scheme? Energy-conserving, structure-preserving methods are how to find out.

  • PIC
  • Structure-Preserving
  • Numerical Heating
05

What Does It Cost?

An energy-conserving scheme needs a global implicit solve where explicit PIC needs none, and GPUs favour explicit PIC. Whether larger timesteps pay for the solve has to be measured on real hardware.

  • HPC
  • GPU
  • Profiling
06

Machine-Learning Closures

My current research: can data-driven closure models reproduce kinetic plasma behavior in affordable fluid simulations? A heat-flux closure and a heating prescription are the same kind of object.

  • Machine Learning
  • Closure Models
  • Kinetic
